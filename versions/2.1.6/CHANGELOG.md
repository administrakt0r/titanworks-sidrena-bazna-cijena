# Titanworks Bazna Cijena 2.1.6

* New: services price list (USLUGE). Paid delivery and other services get their own list, feed and table on the price-list page. WooCommerce shipping methods can be imported and stay in sync (price, name, tax, enabled state).
* New: a new or newly published product (including older drafts) gets its base price automatically from its regular price, dated the first day it is offered. A base-price check finds missing or misdated items and repairs only those with proof.
* New: setup wizard (company and OIB, categories, prices, services, storefront, checks), a tidier admin menu and settings, and an opt-in author credit on the price-list pages. The admin now says "Bazna cijena" everywhere.
* Fix: the public price list shows the statutory columns with each item's base-price date; the dashboard no longer counts variable products as missing; old-format files no longer raise errors forever; a successful manual run clears the "failed" status; no PHP notice on product edit.
